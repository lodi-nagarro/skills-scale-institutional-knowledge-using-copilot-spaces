# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Tech Leads set technical direction, keep architecture aligned, and support the team in delivering sustainable, high-quality solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Set technical direction and align work with architecture standards
- Identify and manage technical risks
- Support estimation and sequencing of work
- Mentor Developers and enforce quality gates (reviews, standards)

### Goals
- Keep delivery technically sound and predictable
- Grow team capability and reduce technical debt

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Estimation and risk reviews in planning

### Key Interfaces
- **Project Managers:** partner on delivery risks and trade-offs
- **Product Managers:** align on scope feasibility and sequencing
- **Developers:** guide implementation standards

---

## QA Lead / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Test Engineers define how quality is verified and provide confidence that work meets acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test strategy and regression coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria
- Triage defects and track quality trends
- Provide release quality sign-off recommendations

### Goals
- Catch defects early and prevent regressions
- Make release readiness clear and evidence-based

### Typical Communication
- Defect triage and test status updates
- Release readiness summaries

### Key Interfaces
- **Developers:** testability and defect triage
- **Product Managers:** validate acceptance criteria
- **Project Managers:** test milestones and readiness
- **UX/UI Designers:** usability and acceptance scenarios

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Platform Engineers keep build, deployment, and runtime infrastructure reliable so teams can ship safely and often.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and environment readiness
- Automate deployments and observability hooks
- Prepare rollback and recovery procedures

### Goals
- Reliable, repeatable releases
- Fast feedback and low deployment risk

### Typical Communication
- Release window coordination
- Pipeline and environment status notices

### Key Interfaces
- **Developers:** support build and deploy workflows
- **Project Managers:** align on release windows and risk mitigation
- **Product Managers:** provide release confidence signals

---

## Security / Compliance Reviewer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Reviewers ensure products meet security and policy requirements and that the team is ready to respond to incidents.

### Responsibilities
- Assess security risks and check policy compliance
- Define secure release criteria
- Recommend incident-readiness measures

### Goals
- Prevent security and compliance issues from reaching production
- Make risks visible early enough to act on them

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk escalations
- Compliance checklists at release gates

### Key Interfaces
- **Developers and Tech Leads:** advise on secure implementation
- **Project Managers:** report risks and escalations
- **Product Managers:** partner on compliance-impact decisions

---

## UX/UI Designer / Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers represent user needs and shape intuitive, accessible experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Create user flows, interaction designs, and design specifications
- Run usability research and validation
- Address accessibility considerations

### Goals
- Deliver usable, accessible experiences that meet user needs
- Reduce rework by validating designs early

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and handoff sessions
- Research readouts

### Key Interfaces
- **Product Managers:** align on outcomes and user needs
- **Developers:** ensure implementation fidelity
- **QA Leads:** define usability and acceptance scenarios

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations Representatives bring real-world customer and operational context into planning and release decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Provide support-readiness input and track known issues
- Share customer-impact feedback
- Identify rollout communication needs

### Goals
- Ensure customers and support teams are prepared for changes
- Shorten the loop from production issues to fixes

### Typical Communication
- Release communication and escalation playbooks
- Regular customer-issue summaries

### Key Interfaces
- **Product Managers:** inform of real-world pain points
- **Project Managers:** coordinate release communications and escalations
- **Developers:** provide production issue context

---

## Data Analyst / Analytics Owner

### Role Summary
Data Analysts and Analytics Owners make outcomes measurable by defining instrumentation and reporting on key metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Define instrumentation and telemetry requirements
- Build KPI reporting and dashboards
- Support experiments and readouts
- Measure outcomes against success metrics

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decisions
- Make success measurable for every release

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and experiment readouts
- Telemetry requirement discussions in planning

### Key Interfaces
- **Product Managers:** partner on success metrics
- **Project Managers:** supply status signals
- **Developers:** guide telemetry requirements

---

## Phase-based collaboration notes
- **Initiation:** Product Managers, Tech Leads, Security, and UX align on goals, feasibility, and risks.
- **Planning:** Tech Leads and QA contribute estimates and test strategy; Data Analysts define metrics and instrumentation.
- **Execution:** Developers, QA, UX, and DevOps collaborate continuously; Project Managers track risks and dependencies.
- **Release:** QA, Security, DevOps, and Support confirm readiness; Project Managers coordinate communications.
- **Retrospective:** All roles review outcomes, metrics, and process improvements.
